# In one minute

The short way through a bulletin, linked from the top of Contents: every story as its short title, its evidence mark and the one sentence that says what happened. Each line opens the full story.

Each line carries a small square of the story's one visual on its right, level with the title: its credited photo, else its chart, map or logo (see [visuals](visuals.md)). A story with none of these shows the logo of the first outlet that reported it, and with no logo either the line stays text only, never a stock image. The square is 84px on phones and 112px on wide screens, so the sentence keeps most of the width.
