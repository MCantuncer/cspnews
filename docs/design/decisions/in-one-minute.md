# In one minute

The short way through a bulletin, linked from the top of Contents. It shows one story at a time, so attention stays on a single thing and the page reads differently from Contents, which is already the scrolling list with thumbnails.

Each story is a card: the story's one visual (its credited photo, else its chart, map or logo, as in [visuals](visuals.md)), the section with its icon, the evidence mark and short title, the one sentence that says what happened, and up to three logos of the outlets that reported it with their count. A story with no visual gets a quiet field in its section colour with the section icon, never a stock image. The card opens the full story.

Cards sit in a row that swipes sideways and snaps to whole cards, which works without a script. Above the row, one thin mark per story fills up to the card in view. Below it, a back button, the count ("3 / 7") and a next button; the arrow keys move between cards too. The last card is the end of the bulletin, with the next edition and the way to the full bulletin.

Photos stand 4:3 on phones and 16:9 on wide screens. Nothing moves on its own: no auto-advance and no timers.
