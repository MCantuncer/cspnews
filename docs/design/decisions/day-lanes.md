# Day lanes

Under a bulletin's title: **When each story broke**. Wide screens (1100 px and up) draw one lane per section across the full content width, one dot per story at its first report, from the hour before the day's first story to the bulletin's own time. The scale ends at a vertical line in the hour colour labelled with that time and the word "bulletin", so a quiet stretch before it reads as "nothing new broke", never as a chart cut short. Every story's title is written beside its dot: just above or below the line first, then a row further out, as many rows as it takes so none overlap, with a hairline from the dot to a title that sits further out. A developing story's dot carries a soft ring.

Narrow screens show the same day as a list in time order (time, section dot, title), ending with the bulletin in the hour colour. Every dot and row opens its story.
