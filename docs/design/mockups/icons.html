Our own icons, three ways

Each direction is a complete set on a 24 grid with one 1.75 stroke, shown at 16, 20 and 24 in both themes, then placed in real rows. Evidence colour is never alone: every mark has its own shape.

Recommended: Stations. It is the Meridian itself, so evidence, controls and sections all read as stops on the day line, and filled vs open survives 16px better than the other two.

1

Stations recommended

Every mark is a station on a short stretch of the day line: filled means settled, open means waiting, a broken line means the record does not agree with itself.

Round caps, one 1.75 stroke, 4.5 station radius everywhere, so the icons read as pieces of the same Meridian you see on the home screen.

Cut corners

Built from Funnel’s own squared-off bowl: a superellipse with three soft corners and one sharp one, the way the typeface flattens its curves.

Butt caps and mitred joins, so the icons sit beside Funnel Display headlines like extra glyphs of the face.

3

Marks

Typographic: each icon is a glyph you could set in a line of text. Evidence becomes = − 1 ≠ and an open blank, so it reads as punctuation, never as a badge.

Diagonal strokes end in Funnel’s flat, horizontal terminal cuts; dots are square, like the face’s i and period.

4

Toggle options

The first toggle read as a slider. These three keep the station but are unmistakably switches: a 44×24 track inside a 52×44 tap area, and on differs from off by knob position, fill and a centre mark, not by colour alone. Tap to try; motion is off under reduced motion.

Recommended: a. Day line. It has the familiar switch silhouette and the filled station is the same “settled” signal as Confirmed.
